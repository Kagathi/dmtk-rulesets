# GURPS (basic)

## 1.11.0

- **Condition bands.** The profile declares how a creature looks as it takes damage, read off
  its HP: Unhurt, Hurt, **Reeling** (less than 1/3 HP left), **Collapsing** (0 HP or less) —
  GURPS Lite’s own thresholds. A player whose table hides creature numbers sees the word.
- Additive. Needs app 1.0.0, which knows the field.

## 1.10.0

- The Equipment and Weapons tables are now **item targets**: a catalog item can be added straight
  into them, with its values landing in the right columns (TL, LC, weight, cost and the rest).
  Additive declaration only — no mechanics changed, and nothing on an existing sheet moves.

## 1.9.0

- Dodge, Parry, and DR are now surfaced as encounter defense stats, so they show at a glance on
  the DM's combat-tracker rows and stat panels. Additive display hint only — no mechanics changed.

## 1.8.0

- Equipment is now a **table** (Item · TL · LC · Qty · Weight · Cost · Notes · page-ref)
  instead of a plain name list, so imported GURPS gear carries full detail. Loot grants
  append a row (keyed by the item name) rather than a string.
