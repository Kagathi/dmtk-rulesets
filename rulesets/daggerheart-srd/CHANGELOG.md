## 1.3.0

**Needs DM Toolkit 0.13.0 or later.**

- **Hit Points, Stress and Armor Slots count up.** You mark them, so a fresh character or
  adversary reads **0 marked**, damage marks and healing clears, and an untouched adversary no
  longer looks maxed out.
- **Active Armor is a table**: name, Major and Severe thresholds, score and feature, each in its
  own column, and armor from the catalog can be added straight onto it. Anything typed into the
  old Active Armor box stays saved on the character; it just isn't shown.
- **Weapons gain a Burden column.**
- **The Loadout and Vault take cards from the catalog**, ready for domain cards.
- **A suggested DM Screen:** conditions, core rules, the Fear pool and the party, with no
  initiative panel, because the game takes turns freely. The rules panels fill in once content is
  installed.
- The adversary template's unused **Fear Features** field is gone.

*1.2.0 is delisted.* It stays in place for anyone who already has it, and an install of it sees
1.3.0 as an update.

## 1.2.0

**Needs DM Toolkit 0.13.0 or later.**

- **A new name.** Darrington Press's licence does not allow its game's name in the title of a
  work, so this ruleset is now **DMTK Ruleset — Spotlight**, and a one-line summary says which game it
  plays wherever rulesets are listed in the app.
- **The licence's full attribution travels inside the file**, in the form the licence asks for,
  including what we changed.
- **Condition reminders in our own words.** The three reminders now say what the condition does
  without following the reference document's wording.
- Mechanics unchanged from 1.1.0.

*1.0.0 and 1.1.0 are delisted* — they stay in place for anyone who already has them, and an
install of either sees 1.2.0 as an update in DM Toolkit 0.13.0.

## 1.1.0

- **Free turn order**, the way the game plays: no initiative column or round counter, and no
  "on deck" on players' phones.
- **The GM's Fear, kept for you:** starts at one per PC in the party, stops at twelve, carries
  between sessions.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the character sheet in the system's own shape, and the adversary stat block
  pre-taught so a bestiary imports without teaching the app its vocabulary first.
