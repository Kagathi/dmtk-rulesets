# Daggerheart (SRD)

Built from the **Daggerheart System Reference Document 2.0**, published by Darrington
Press as Public Game Content under the Darrington Press Community Gaming License.
© 2026 Critical Role LLC.

**Mechanics only.** This is a character-sheet definition: field names, track sizes and
the arithmetic between them. It carries no rule text, no card text and no setting.
Daggerheart is a trademark of Critical Role LLC; this is an unofficial, community-made

## 1.1.0

**Needs DM Toolkit 0.13.0 or later.** Older versions keep seeing 1.0.0, which still works.

- **Takes turns the way Daggerheart does — freely.** The encounter window drops its initiative
  column and round counter, and your players stop seeing "on deck": the GM decides who has
  the spotlight next, so nobody is promised a turn the table has not given them. Give anyone
  the turn with the ▶ on their row.
- **The GM's Fear, kept for you.** A counter in the encounter window and on the DM Screen. It
  starts at **one Fear per PC in the party**, as the SRD says, stops at **twelve**, and carries
  between sessions. It never reaches a player's phone.

## 1.0.0

- **The sheet, in the system’s own shape** rather than a fantasy sheet with the words
  changed. The six traits; **Hope** and **Stress** as real tracks beside Hit Points;
  **Armor Slots** you mark to soak a hit; your **loadout of five domain cards** kept
  separate from the **vault**, because a vaulted card explicitly cannot help you and one
  combined list would lose the only fact about a card that matters mid-scene;
  **Experiences** carrying their modifier as a number you can actually add; and gold as
  **handfuls, bags and chests**, the way the sheet counts it.
- **Two numbers worked out for you**, chosen because they are the two a table forgets:
  your **tier**, from your level, and your **damage thresholds**, which add your level to
  your armour’s base every time you level up.
- **Adversaries come with it.** The stat block is the printed one’s own fields, and the
  importer is pre-taught its vocabulary — Difficulty, Thresholds, HP, Stress, ATK,
  Experience, Motives & Tactics — so a Daggerheart bestiary imports without anyone
  teaching the app what those words mean first, and a creature reaches the combat
  tracker with its hit points.
- **No initiative order**, deliberately: Daggerheart has none, so the sheet does not
  invent one.

**Not included yet:** the adversary and domain-card content itself. This release is the
sheet; a content package is the natural next step and rides the same registry.
