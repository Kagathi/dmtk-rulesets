# D&D 5e SRD content

## 1.2.0

- The equipment is now **catalog gear** as well as reference cards: every weapon, armor and
  adventuring item can be picked straight onto a sheet, stocked in a shopkeeper’s shop, and
  dropped by a loot table. The cards are unchanged — each item and its card come from one
  declaration, so they can’t disagree about what a longsword costs.
- Weapons land in the Weapons table (name, damage, cost, weight); armor and gear land in
  Inventory. The attack bonus is deliberately left blank — it belongs to the character, not
  to the weapon.
- **Needs dnd5e-basic 1.14.0 or newer**, which is the ruleset that first says where items go.

## 1.1.0

- Added the complete SRD 5.2.1 magic items (potions, rings, rods, staffs, wands, wondrous items, and named armor and weapons — with rarity, attunement, and full rules text).
- Added mundane equipment: every weapon (damage, properties, mastery), every armor, and notable adventuring gear.
- Added DM-screen reference cards: Setting a DC, Skills by Ability, Vision & Light, and Travel Pace.

## 1.0.0

- Initial registry publication: all SRD conditions, actions, common rules, and spells, plus the complete bestiary.
